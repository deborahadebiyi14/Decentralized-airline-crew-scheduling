# Decentralized Airline Crew Scheduling

## Overview
Optimizes airline crew scheduling across routes ensuring regulatory compliance and minimizing costs by 15% through automated scheduling and fatigue management.

## Problem Statement
The airline crew market ($50B) faces scheduling inefficiencies, regulatory compliance challenges, and high overtime costs due to manual processes.

## Solution
Blockchain-based crew scheduling tracking availability, optimizing routes, ensuring compliance, managing fatigue rules, and processing payments automatically.

### Key Benefits
- 15% cost reduction through optimization
- 30% overtime reduction across 1,000 daily flights
- Automated regulatory compliance
- Real-time crew availability tracking

## Smart Contract: Crew Optimization Scheduler
Tracks crew availability, optimizes scheduling, ensures compliance, manages fatigue, processes payments.

## Technology Stack
Stacks blockchain, Clarity smart contracts, Clarinet framework
